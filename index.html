A gentle night-garden puzzle

Bring the stars
back to life.

Guide Pip, Mallow, and Lumi through a moonlit garden where every step changes the path of the friends behind you.

Drag Pip across hexagonal stones. Mallow follows when the links stretch, and Lumi lights the stars she enters. Find the right route through 20 handcrafted levels, opening bridges and moving stone orbs as the puzzles grow.

Three friends, one route

Plan each move around how the whole little team follows.

Thoughtful twists

Switches, bridges, and stone orbs make every new garden worth exploring.

Play at your pace

No timer. Revisit levels, improve your star rating, and enjoy the music or turn it off.
