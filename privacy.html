Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy describes the privacy practices of Firefly Relay, a game provided by Wang Cong.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your completed levels, best star ratings, and music and sound-effect preferences in local app storage. This information helps the game remember your progress and settings. An unfinished level is not saved when the app is closed.

Information we collect or share

The current version does not require an account or ask for personal information. It does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not send gameplay progress or settings to us. We do not sell or share personal information collected through the game.

Permissions and tracking

The current version does not request permission to track you across apps or websites. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. Audio is played from files included with the game.

Retention and deletion

Progress and settings remain in the app's local storage until the app and its local data are removed. You can remove that data by deleting the app from your device. Device backups, if enabled, are managed by your operating system and its settings.

Children

Firefly Relay does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date before those changes are reflected in the app.
